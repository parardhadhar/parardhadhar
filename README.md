### Flagship: [AI Crystallography Engine — TEM AI Studio](https://github.com/parardhadhar/AI-Crystallography-Engine)

**Machine learning × computer vision × materials science.** This is one of the projects I'm most proud of: turning complex electron-microscopy workflows into an integrated research tool.

- **Diffusion + detection:** PyTorch denoising diffusion models for noisy lattice reconstruction, improving downstream YOLOv11 defect detection accuracy by **45%**.
- **Scientific computing:** OpenCV-based **2D FFT analysis** and geometric **Burgers vector calculations** for **50+ megapixel scans**.
- **End-to-end engineering:** **FastAPI** inference with a **React/Electron** interface and an **LLM copilot** with streaming responses.
- **Research impact:** Reduced manual defect calculation from **2+ hours to under 15 seconds per micrograph**, during my **IISc Bangalore research internship**.
